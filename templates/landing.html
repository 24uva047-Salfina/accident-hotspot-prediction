Welcome to Accident Hotspot Prediction

Machine Learning-Based Accident Hotspot Prediction and Risk Classification Using GIS

This project identifies accident-prone areas using historical accident data, machine learning techniques and GIS-based spatial analysis. It helps classify locations into Low, Medium and High accident risk levels.

GIS-Based Mapping

Visualize accident-prone locations and risk levels on an interactive map using GIS.

Machine Learning

LightGBM is used to analyze accident-related features and predict accident risk levels.

Risk Classification

Locations are classified into Low, Medium and High risk categories for better analysis.
